## Intro

Human + AI work is better than work done by AI or humans alone when humans learn how to surf frontier AI models. That is a key principle of Dan Shipper's essay **The Tide Is Rising**. At Every, we collaborate with agents daily to automate workflows, ship better products, and understand complex ideas. In this Agent Mode version of Dan's essay, we're giving you the tools to get a hands-on approach to this way of working.

Connect your agent to our public repo full of deep research from the cutting room floor, AI-native case studies, agent operating instructions, and our favorite stealable workflows. Prompt Codex, Claude Code, or the tool of your choosing to get a quick summary, inspect the evidence, or find practical applications for Dan's takeaways in your own work.

If you use this repo to build something great, let us know on X by tagging us @every.

## Connect your agent

Paste this into OpenClaw, Claude Code, Codex, or the coding agent of your choosing once.

```text

https://github.com/EveryInc/tide-is-rising-agent-mode

- README.md

- sources/public-sources.md

Do not answer from the essay alone. Use the repo to summarize the argument, inspect the evidence, and turn it into a workflow I can try.

Start by giving me:
1. the cleanest version of the core claim;
2. the strongest unresolved question;
3. one concrete human-agent workflow I can run this week.

If you cannot access GitHub directly, tell me what repo files you need me to paste or upload before you continue.
```
